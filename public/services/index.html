What we do

CONVERSION

Legacy code conversion

Software written for machines nobody makes anymore, carried over to a modern language and platform — with the original behavior documented and tested against the old system.

MODERNIZATION

Industrial system modernization

Keep the machine, replace the computer. We bridge old process control and instrumentation to modern hardware and interfaces, on your schedule and around your downtime.

SUPPORT

Legacy system support

Board-level repair, spares, and contract maintenance to keep a system running until its replacement is ready.

MIGRATION

Data recovery & format migration

Source code, databases and logs pulled off old tape, floppies and disk packs, and converted into formats your current systems can read.

ADVISORY

Assessment & consulting

Risk reviews of equipment you still depend on, modernization roadmaps, and valuations for insurance, estates and acquisitions.

ONSITE

Onsite work

When the system cannot leave the plant floor or the lab, we come to you, Chicagoland and beyond.

How an engagement runs

01

Describe it

Tell us what the system does, what it runs on, and what’s driving the change. A photo and a paragraph is usually enough to start.

02

Free assessment

We look at the code, the hardware and the process around it, and tell you what’s realistic — no charge, no obligation.

03

Written quote

Fixed scope, fixed price, agreed deliverables and cutover plan. NDA in place before work starts.

04

Convert, verify, hand over

We run old and new side by side until the results match, then deliver the system with documentation of what was done and what we recommend next.
