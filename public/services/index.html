What we handle

RECOVERY

Data recovery from tape

9-track, 7-track and cartridge formats read on our own drives — including reels other labs have already declined.

RECOVERY

Floppy & disk pack imaging

8-inch through 3.5-inch, plus removable packs. Flux-level capture where the media is marginal.

MIGRATION

Format conversion & migration

Legacy databases, word processor files and instrument logs turned into formats your current systems can read.

SUPPORT

Legacy system repair & support

Board-level repair, spares, and contract maintenance for systems long past vendor support.

ENGINEERING

Industrial & lab modernization

Keep the machine, replace the computer. We bridge old process control and instrumentation to modern interfaces on your schedule.

ADVISORY

Consulting & appraisal

Valuations for insurance, estates and acquisitions; risk reviews of equipment you still depend on.

ONSITE

Onsite work

When the system cannot leave the building or the media cannot leave the room, we come to you, Chicagoland and beyond.

How an engagement runs

01

Describe it

A photo and a paragraph is usually enough for us to tell you what is realistic.

02

Free evaluation

We inspect the media or system and report what can be read, repaired or migrated — no charge, no obligation.

03

Written quote

Fixed scope, fixed price, agreed deliverable format. NDA and chain-of-custody log in place before work starts.

04

Deliver & document

Files on the media you choose plus a written record of what was done, what failed, and what we recommend next.
