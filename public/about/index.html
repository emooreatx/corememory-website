About

One shop, two jobs, the same skill.

The community side is the heart: open hours, classes, restoration nights, school groups, and the chance to stand in front of a working SEL 810A instead of a photo of one. The restoration service is the engine: recovering data from dead media, converting legacy formats, and keeping systems alive that businesses and labs still depend on.

What ties them together is credibility. The machines on our floor prove we can do the paid work, and the paid work keeps the floor open, lit and free to walk into. That’s the whole plan — we keep old computing alive, for people and for businesses.
